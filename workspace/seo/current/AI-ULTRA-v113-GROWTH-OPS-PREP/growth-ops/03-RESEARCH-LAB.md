# AI ULTRA Research Lab — operating model

## Goal
Create primary-source assets that journalists, specialists, partners and AI/search systems can cite because the data or analysis cannot be obtained by copying generic articles.

## What counts as a research asset
- survey with documented sample;
- public-data audit with reproducible criteria;
- benchmark with controlled test conditions;
- experiment with repeatable protocol;
- anonymized aggregate from real project work, where permissions allow;
- longitudinal dataset updated on a fixed cadence.

## Publication template
1. Research question.
2. Why it matters.
3. Scope/sample and dates.
4. Methodology.
5. Raw/aggregated data policy.
6. Results.
7. Interpretation.
8. Limitations and what the study cannot prove.
9. Practical implications.
10. Downloadable chart/table/data if safe.
11. Author/reviewer/date/version.
12. Citation block: canonical title + URL + date.

## First research backlog
High priority after launch:
1. Moscow AI adoption audit: public websites of 100–250 companies across 5–10 sectors.
2. AI-agent ROI benchmark: modeled + real anonymized cases, clearly separated.
3. AI sales response-time benchmark: human workflow vs AI-assisted workflow.
4. Russian-language AI call-center benchmark: latency, task completion, handoff quality, cost.
5. AI Search/GEO readiness audit of Moscow B2B websites.
6. Yandex SERP study for commercial AI/automation queries in Moscow.
7. CRM + AI integration failure modes from real implementations.
8. LLM comparison for specific Russian business tasks using a fixed evaluation rubric.

## Evidence rule
Never present modeled numbers as observed real-world results. Mark each data point as one of:
- measured;
- survey-reported;
- public-source observation;
- modeled scenario;
- expert judgment.

## Citation earning workflow
Research page on AI ULTRA → visual charts → concise press brief → targeted pitches → expert posts on relevant platforms → journalist responses → follow-up with raw methodology → earned mentions/backlinks.

## Success metrics
- editorial citations;
- unique referring domains;
- branded searches after release;
- newsletter/lead capture from research;
- assisted commercial conversions;
- citations in AI/search answers where observable.
