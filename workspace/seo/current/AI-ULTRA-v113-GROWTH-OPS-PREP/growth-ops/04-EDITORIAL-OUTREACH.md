# Editorial / PR distribution playbook

## Principle
One research asset becomes many editorial angles, but the full primary source remains on AI ULTRA. Do not syndicate identical copy everywhere.

## Priority channels
- Habr — technical implementation, benchmarks, engineering lessons.
- VC.ru — founder/business angle, experiments, cases, market observations.
- RB.RU — expert columns, business/technology data.
- Workspace — digital/agency/SEO/implementation cases.
- Cossa / Sostav — marketing, advertising, conversion, AI in marketing.
- Pressfeed — answer journalist requests with data and expert commentary.
- TenChat — expert distribution and short-form professional commentary.
- SearchEngines.Guru — genuine SEO experiments/discussion, not link spam.
- LinkedIn / Medium / DEV — international/technical distribution after Russian core is stable.
- GitHub — code, methodology assets, datasets/demos where appropriate.

## Pitch formula
Subject: one concrete data point, not “we want to publish an article”.

Pitch body:
- What was studied.
- Sample/date/method.
- 2–4 counterintuitive findings.
- Why their audience should care now.
- Link to primary source / methodology.
- Offer of a custom comment, chart or raw aggregate.

## Backlink rule
Ask for attribution when our original data is used, but do not demand keyword-stuffed anchors. A natural citation to the exact research URL is preferable.

## Forbidden shortcuts
- bulk forum profile links;
- unmoderated directories;
- paid link exchanges;
- mass guest posts with recycled text;
- fake expert accounts;
- invented research numbers.
