# IMPORTANT REMINDER — PAID TRAFFIC

When work starts on SMM, Yandex Direct or paid acquisition, STOP before campaign launch and confirm all five items:

1. What exact landing page matches the audience/query?
2. What valuable PDF/checklist/template does the visitor receive?
3. What Metrica goal measures the gift conversion?
4. What follow-up happens after the download?
5. How do we measure qualified leads rather than only downloads?

Default rule: **paid traffic should arrive with a useful reason to engage immediately.**

Preferred first gifts:
- AI-ready sales audit checklist;
- ROI AI-agent worksheet;
- AI automation opportunity map;
- Yandex + AI Search visibility checklist;
- AI-agent brief template.
