# Measurement taxonomy

## North-star categories
1. Search visibility.
2. Qualified demand.
3. Trust/authority.
4. Content usefulness.
5. Paid-acquisition efficiency.

## Search KPIs
- indexed production URLs / submitted URLs;
- query impressions/clicks by cluster;
- Moscow query visibility separately from Russia/CIS;
- branded vs non-branded demand;
- landing-page CTR;
- crawling/indexing errors;
- Yandex quality indicators / recommendations.

## Conversion events
- cta_click
- telegram_click
- contact_submit
- consultation_start
- qualified_lead
- portfolio_open
- case_read_50
- case_read_90
- pricing_open
- lead_magnet_view
- lead_magnet_download
- research_download
- research_citation_outreach_click (internal CRM/outreach tracking, not public analytics)

## Content/authority KPIs
- unique referring domains;
- editorial mentions without links;
- citations to research pages;
- case-study assisted conversions;
- repeat visitors;
- branded search growth;
- direct traffic trend;
- journalist/partner inbound requests.

## UTM standard
utm_source = platform/publisher
utm_medium = cpc|social|referral|email|pr
utm_campaign = stable_campaign_name
utm_content = creative_or_article_angle
utm_term = paid_search_keyword_or_audience

Do not change naming mid-campaign without a migration note.
