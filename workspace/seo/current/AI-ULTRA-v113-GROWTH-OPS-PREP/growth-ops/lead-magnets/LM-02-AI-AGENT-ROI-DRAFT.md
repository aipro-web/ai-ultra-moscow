# Draft lead magnet — AI Agent ROI worksheet

Working title: **Как посчитать ROI AI-сотрудника до внедрения**

Inputs:
- monthly interaction volume;
- current human minutes per interaction;
- loaded hourly labor cost;
- current missed/late-response rate;
- expected automation/assist share;
- AI/integration/support cost;
- escalation share;
- expected revenue or cost-saving impact where measurable.

Output scenarios:
- conservative;
- base;
- upside.

Important: modeled scenarios must never be presented as guaranteed client results. After launch, build a calculator from this worksheet and validate assumptions against real projects.
