# Lead Magnet System

## Project rule
When AI ULTRA prepares SMM, Yandex Direct or other paid acquisition, ALWAYS ask:
**What useful asset does the visitor receive immediately after arriving?**
Do not run a generic “visit our site” campaign when a relevant high-value lead magnet can be offered.

## Lead magnet quality standard
A PDF/checklist must save time or improve a real decision. It should not be a brochure disguised as a gift.

Required structure:
1. One concrete problem.
2. A fast self-diagnosis/checklist.
3. A framework or calculation method.
4. Examples/templates.
5. Clear next action.
6. Transparent AI ULTRA authorship/contact, without hard-sell overload.

## First lead magnets to prepare after core launch
### LM-01 — «25 точек, где бизнес теряет деньги без AI-автоматизации»
Best for: broad AI automation / SMM awareness.
CTA: get checklist → request process audit.

### LM-02 — «Калькулятор ROI AI-сотрудника: формула и таблица для руководителя»
Best for: AI agents / sales / call center.
CTA: download model → calculate with AI ULTRA.

### LM-03 — «AI-ready аудит отдела продаж: 40 вопросов»
Best for: Moscow B2B / Yandex Direct.
CTA: self-score → book diagnostic.

### LM-04 — «Яндекс + AI Search: checklist видимости сайта на 2027»
Best for: SEO/GEO campaigns.
CTA: checklist → full site audit.

### LM-05 — «ТЗ на AI-агента без технического языка»
Best for: warm commercial intent.
CTA: fill template → send it to AI ULTRA for estimate.

## Campaign mapping rule
Each campaign/ad group should map:
query/audience → landing intent → lead magnet → CTA → Metrica goal → follow-up script.

## Measurement
Track separately:
- lead_magnet_view
- lead_magnet_start
- lead_magnet_download
- lead_magnet_submit
- lead_magnet_to_consultation
- qualified_lead

Do not judge a lead magnet only by download count. The primary business metric is qualified-lead contribution.
