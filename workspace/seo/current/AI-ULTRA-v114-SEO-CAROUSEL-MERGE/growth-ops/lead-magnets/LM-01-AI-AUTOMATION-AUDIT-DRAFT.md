# Draft lead magnet — AI automation opportunity audit

Working title: **25 точек, где бизнес теряет время и деньги без AI-автоматизации**

## Self-check sections
- Incoming leads and response time.
- Qualification and routing.
- Repetitive customer questions.
- CRM hygiene and data entry.
- Follow-ups and missed callbacks.
- Knowledge retrieval.
- Reporting/analytics preparation.
- Content reuse/distribution.
- Internal approvals.
- Customer retention/reactivation.

For each point ask:
- How often does this happen?
- How many minutes/hours are spent manually?
- What error/lead-loss risk exists?
- Can AI act autonomously or only assist?
- What system must be integrated?
- What human approval remains necessary?

End result: priority matrix Impact × Frequency × Automation feasibility.
