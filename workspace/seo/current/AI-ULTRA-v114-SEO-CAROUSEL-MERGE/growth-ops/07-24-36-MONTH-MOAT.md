# 24–36 month moat

## Asset classes AI ULTRA should own
- authoritative service pages;
- real portfolio/case library;
- Research Lab datasets and benchmarks;
- repeatable calculators/audit utilities;
- expert author profiles and editorial history;
- partner/media citation graph;
- video/demo library;
- technical/open assets where appropriate;
- branded newsletter / subscriber audience;
- recurring benchmark reports people expect each year.

## Compounding loop
Real projects → observations → cases → research questions → data → research report → editorial distribution → citations/links → branded demand → more projects → better data.

## Strategic outcome
The defensible advantage is not page count. It is accumulated evidence, proprietary data, recognizable expertise, useful tools, brand demand and a citation history that competitors cannot reproduce quickly.
