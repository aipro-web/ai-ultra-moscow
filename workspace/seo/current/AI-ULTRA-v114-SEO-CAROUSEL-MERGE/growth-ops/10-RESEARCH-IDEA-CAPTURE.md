# Research idea capture

Use this whenever a real-world observation or debate appears during work.

- Observation / question:
- Why it may be true:
- Why it matters to business:
- What would falsify it:
- Data we already have:
- Data we could collect ethically:
- Sample definition:
- Measurement rule:
- Bias/confounders:
- Potential article angle:
- Potential journalist/industry audience:
- Potential commercial connection (without forcing one):
- Decision: discard / note / pilot / full study / recurring benchmark.

The goal is not to prove the founder's opinion. The goal is to design a test that can reveal whether the opinion is right, wrong or context-dependent.
